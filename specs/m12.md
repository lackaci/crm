# M12 — Drafts, then allowlisted sending

## Goal
Create drafts first, then add tightly controlled sending only after an explicit
owner approval, with duplicate protection and audit evidence.

## Exact files or areas expected to change
- `app/providers/email_base.py` and the Gmail provider: add draft/send
  operations behind the interface.
- `app/reply/approval.py` and draft workflow: require an explicit Approve click.
- `app/db/drafts.py` and audit code: track state, approval, duplicate checks,
  and one audit entry per send.
- `app/ui/approval.py` and `app/ui/approval_queue.py`: expose draft review and
  approval without accidental sending.
- Tests for draft creation, approval, allowlisting, duplicate prevention, and
  audit records.

## Acceptance criteria
- The first stage creates a provider draft and never sends it.
- Sending cannot occur without an explicit owner Approve action.
- While testing, sends are accepted only to the hard-coded owner/test
  allowlist; another recipient is rejected.
- A repeated approval or retry cannot send the same draft twice.
- Each send has an audit entry, and a failed send leaves the draft pending.
- Tests use fakes or the owner’s test address only; no customer mail is sent.

## Security/safety constraints
- Sending is disabled before M12 and remains disabled in Phase 1.
- Keep the allowlist enforced in business code, not only in the UI.
- Preserve escalation rules: unclear, risky, or unapproved replies cannot send.
- Keep credentials in the approved secret store and do not log message bodies,
  tokens, or other secrets.

## Out of scope
- Any sending implementation during Phase 1.
- General auto-send, category-based auto-send, or removal of owner approval.
- Attachment intelligence, payments, calendar, or accounting integrations.
- Public production Gmail rollout or provider details not specified in PLAN2.
